How we train

Every session follows the same shape: a warm-up with the ball, a technical block, a game-related practice, then a small-sided match. Coaches stop the play to coach, not to shout.

What to bring

Boots (moulded studs are fine on our grass), shin pads, a water bottle and the gold training kit. In winter we add a green base layer. Jewellery stays off. Parents wait in the clubhouse, not on the touchline, so players can focus.
