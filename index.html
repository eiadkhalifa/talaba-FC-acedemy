Train with purpose. Play with pride.

Talaba-FC is a community academy in Birmingham. We coach boys and girls from 8 to 18, from first touches to first-team habits.

Welcome to the academy

Talaba means students. That is how we treat every player who walks through the gate: as a learner first, and a footballer always. Sessions are run by FA-qualified coaches on a full-size grass pitch at Riverside Sports Ground.

Whether your child is new to the game or already playing for a school side, we keep groups small, feedback clear, and the atmosphere competitive but kind. Match play starts from U11. Younger groups focus on movement, passing and confidence on the ball.

What you will find here

Players aged 8–18

Four age pathways, from Foundations to the senior Pathway group. Siblings can train on the same evening.

Qualified coaching

Every lead coach holds at least an FA Level 2 award. Goalkeepers get a dedicated session each Thursday.

One home ground

All training and home matches take place at Riverside Sports Ground, ten minutes from Birmingham city centre.
