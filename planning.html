Who the site is for

This site is for parents and carers of children aged 8 to 18 in Birmingham, and for older players who want to join on their own. They need to know quickly whether Talaba-FC is the right academy: the age groups we take, what it costs each month, which kit to buy, where Riverside Sports Ground is, and how to book a trial without chasing someone on the phone. Coaches also need a public timetable they can point families to. The pages are written in plain English, with fees, session times and an enquiry form on the surface rather than hidden in a brochure. Visitors can move from any page to any other page, so a parent who lands on Contact Us can still check prices before they send the form.
