About Talaba-FC

The academy opened in 2019 after a group of local coaches grew tired of long waiting lists at bigger clubs. We wanted a place where a shy eight-year-old and a driven seventeen-year-old could both improve in the same colours.

Today we train around 140 players. Fees stay in the game: floodlights, first-aid cover, DBS-checked staff and a kit that players can be proud to wear at school on a Friday.

Location

Riverside Sports Ground

42 Greenfield Lane
Birmingham
B12 8QT

The ground sits beside the River Rea, a short walk from Balsall Heath. Bus routes 35 and 50 stop on Highgate Road. There is free parking for 40 cars beside the clubhouse.

Training evenings: gates open 17:30. Saturday mornings: gates open 08:45.

Prices

Fees are paid monthly by bank transfer. The first month includes a two-session trial. If the player decides not to stay, we refund that month in full.

Pathway 17–18 2 plus gym access £58

Match-day kit pack (home shirt, shorts, socks): £55 once per season. Training kit: £28. Goalkeeper gloves are not included.
